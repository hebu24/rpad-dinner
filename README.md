# R-PAD Dinner

**OpenReview for food around CMU.** Restaurant rankings and reviews near Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, from members of the R-PAD (Robots Perceiving and Doing) Lab.

**Live site:** https://hebu24.github.io/rpad-dinner/

Features a ranked list of restaurants, food trucks and campus dining near CMU (Oakland, Shadyside, Squirrel Hill and more); walk, bus and Uber travel times from Newell-Simon Hall with Google Maps directions; and OpenReview-style ratings (Strong Accept to Strong Reject) with strengths, weaknesses and comments.

Reviews can be posted by approved R-PAD members. Learn about the lab at https://r-pad.github.io/
